Using Steady with your psychologist

Steady lets you check in between sessions and complete questionnaires or exercises your psychologist sends you. Your practice decides how your information is used for your care. OpenWorks IT, which makes Steady, runs the practice's Steady server for it.

What we collect
- Your name and email address, so you can sign in.
- Your check-ins: four ratings from 1 to 5 and anything you choose to write.
- Your answers to questionnaires and exercises your psychologist sends you.
- Technical information: which version of the app you use and on what kind of phone, when you last opened it, your time zone, and a token so your phone can receive notifications.
- The internet address your phone connects from, kept in the practice's access log for security.
- Only if you choose to connect a watch: a daily summary of your heart rate, sleep, steps and blood oxygen. You are asked separately before this starts, and you can stop it at any time in Settings.

Who can see it
- Your psychologist.
- A colleague at the practice, if your psychologist shares your record with them (for example while they are away).
- The practice's master psychologist and administrator see your name and whether you are using the app, not what you write. They can see the access log: who opened your record, and when.
- OpenWorks IT (Pty) Ltd runs the practice's server in South Africa on the practice's behalf. It can technically reach the server to keep it running, does not read your information, and is bound to keep it confidential.
Notifications reach Android phones through Google and iPhones through Apple. They only ever say "New notification": never your name, what you wrote or what it is about.
Your information is not sold or used for advertising.

Your clinical record
Your psychologist may export what you share in Steady, watch data included, and add it to your clinical record. From then on it is part of your clinical record: the practice keeps and protects it as the law requires, and your psychologist decides how to use it in your care.

Why
To support your treatment. Your psychologist uses it alongside your sessions. Some answers are scored to help your psychologist review how you are doing, and your psychologist may compare your check-ins and watch data with your own usual pattern, to improve your care. Scores are not a diagnosis.

This app is not an emergency service
Nobody watches the app around the clock. Your psychologist will usually see what you send during working hours. If you need help now, use the crisis numbers shown in the app.

How long it is kept
Your psychologist keeps your clinical records for as long as the law requires (HPCSA Booklet 9). If you ask to delete your Steady account, the practice does so within 30 days, after copying what belongs in your clinical file; encrypted backup copies are overwritten within a further 30 days.

If you are under 18
If you are 12 or older and understand what this means, you can agree to it yourself; your psychologist will talk it through with you first. If you are younger than 12, a parent or guardian agrees for you.

Your rights
You may ask to see your information, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, subject to the practice's legal duty to keep clinical records. You can stop using the app at any time by telling your psychologist. You may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
