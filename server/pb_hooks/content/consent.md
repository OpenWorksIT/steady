Using Steady with your psychologist

Steady lets you check in between sessions and complete questionnaires or exercises your psychologist sends you.

What we collect
- Your name and email address, so you can sign in.
- Your check-ins: four ratings from 1 to 5 and anything you choose to write.
- Your answers to questionnaires and exercises your psychologist sends you.
- Technical information needed to send you notifications, such as a device token.
- Only if you choose to connect a watch: a daily summary of your heart rate, sleep, steps and blood oxygen. You are asked separately before this starts, and you can stop it at any time in Settings.

Who can see it
Only your psychologist at the practice and the practice's appointed IT operator, who looks after the system under a confidentiality agreement. Your information is stored on servers in South Africa. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Why
To support your treatment. Your psychologist uses it alongside your sessions. Some answers are scored to help your psychologist review how you are doing; scores are not a diagnosis.

This app is not an emergency service
Nobody watches the app around the clock. Your psychologist will usually see what you send during working hours. If you need help now, use the crisis numbers shown in the app.

Your rights
You may ask to see your information, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, subject to the practice's legal duty to keep clinical records. You can stop using the app at any time by telling your psychologist. You may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
