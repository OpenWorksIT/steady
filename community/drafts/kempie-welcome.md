# Welcome from Kempie

Hello, and welcome.

I'm Kempie van Rooyen, a clinical psychologist at VR Clinical Psychology in Gqeberha, and I lead this community.

Steady grew out of my research, and VR's psychologists and patients have shaped it through two rounds of testing. One practice sees its own patients. Several practices, comparing notes, can see much more: which questionnaires work in our setting, which exercises patients actually do, what helps between sessions and what doesn't. That is what this place is for.

A few places to start:

- **Introduce yourself**, in the Tea room. I'll go first. The Tea room is our staff room, for books, CPD and the life around the work.
- **The de-identifying checklist**, pinned in Clinical practice. Read it before you write about a case. The one rule here is no patient-identifying information, ever.
- **The science**, which sets out the thinking behind each part of Steady. Question it: that's how it gets better.
- **Steady development**, if something in Steady gets in your way. OpenWorks IT reads along there.

This isn't a place for urgent clinical matters, and nobody watches it around the clock. It is a place to think out loud with colleagues.

I'm glad you're here.

Kempie
