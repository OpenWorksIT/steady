Welcome to %{site_name}, the community of psychologists at practices that use Steady.

Three places to start:

- **Introduce yourself**, in the Tea room, the community's staff room: a few lines about you and your work. The Tea room is also for books, CPD and what's helping you this month.
- **De-identifying a case before you post: a checklist**, pinned in Clinical practice. The one rule here is no patient-identifying information, ever, in topics and in personal messages alike.
- **The Steady manual**, step by step with screenshots, for when you want to know how something works.
- **The science**, for the thinking behind each part of Steady.

To ask OpenWorks IT, who build Steady, post in Steady development and mention `@openworks`. For a problem with the app itself, the Feedback button in Steady is quickest.

This isn't a place for urgent clinical matters: nobody watches it around the clock.

Kempie van Rooyen leads the community. Send him a personal message if there's anything you need.

(OpenWorks IT's accounts see Announcements, the Steady manual and Steady development only. Practice administrators' accounts see the Steady manual only.)
