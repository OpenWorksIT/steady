Steady Community

For psychologists at practices that use Steady: the science behind between-session care, the questionnaires and tools, and studies across practices.

Run by OpenWorks IT for the practices that use Steady. Members are psychologists, each signed in through their own practice's Steady.

No patient-identifying information, ever. No names, dates of birth, places, dates of sessions or rare details. If you see any, flag the post: the community lead removes it.
