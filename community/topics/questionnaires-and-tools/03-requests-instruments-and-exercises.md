# Requests: instruments and exercises you'd like

Is there a questionnaire you'd like in the library, or an exercise you'd send if Steady had it? Ask here. Requests help decide what goes into Steady for every practice, and someone may already have checked the licence.

## Before you ask

Look in Library > Add > Import. Steady's list from the open ReproSchema library already includes, among others, the PHQ-8, PHQ-4, PHQ-15, PC-PTSD-5, the Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale, PROMIS Global Health and the 12-item WHODAS 2.0, each with Steady's note on its licence.

## What to include

- the full name, and who developed or publishes it
- a link to its official source: a DOI, or the developer's page
- its licence, as far as you can tell (see "The questionnaire library and licences")
- what you'd use it for, and with whom: adults, adolescents, a particular presentation
- the language you need; translations carry their own licences and their own evidence
- for an exercise: the steps or the breathing pattern, and where the wording comes from

## A note on licences

Many well-known instruments are free to use. Some are free only for research, some need permission, and some are sold by publishers. An instrument can go into Steady for every practice only if its licence allows that. A practice that holds its own licence for an instrument can still import it for its own use, and confirms that in the library.

## What Steady can do today

Questionnaires with choice, written and number answers, conditional questions, sub-scales and score bands; step-by-step exercises with space to write; and paced breathing. If what you have in mind needs something else, such as audio or a diary over several days, say so: that's a request for Steady development as well.
