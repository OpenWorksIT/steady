# Publishing a paper: discussed here, then out with a DOI
<!-- steady: pinned -->

Papers written here can go out to everyone: on [steadyapp.co.za/papers](https://steadyapp.co.za/papers/), and on Zenodo, CERN's open research repository, with a DOI that won't change. This forum stays private; the paper doesn't have to.

## How it works

1. **Post the paper** as a topic in Research and studies, titled "Paper:" and its title, with the PDF or Word file attached and the text in the post (paste it, or ask OpenWorks for a clean Markdown copy: tables and all).
2. **Two weeks or more of comments.** Read it as a reviewer would: is the method sound, do the claims follow, does each reference say what it's cited for, what does it mean in our practices? Say what you'd change, and why.
3. **The author answers and revises**, and says in the topic when it's ready, and that it may go out under CC BY 4.0 (anyone may share and adapt it, with credit).
4. **OpenWorks IT checks it**: references and links, that no patient can be identified, that a study with patients' data names its ethics approval, that every questionnaire it reproduces may be reproduced (see "The questionnaire library and licences"), and that what it says about Steady is true of Steady today.
5. **It goes out**: the author deposits it on Zenodo with their ORCID, in the `steadyapp` community that OpenWorks runs, and it gets its own page on steadyapp.co.za with the PDF, the DOI and how to cite it. A reply here links to both.

## What reviewing here is, and isn't

It's colleagues reading carefully, in the open among ourselves. It isn't a journal's peer review, and the published page says so. The comments stay here; the paper says only that it was discussed in the Steady Community, and when.

## Corrections

A correction or update is a new version on Zenodo and on the page, with what changed. Post it here first, like the original.
