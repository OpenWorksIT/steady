# How multi-practice studies will work
<!-- steady: pinned -->

One reason for this community is that several practices, comparing notes, can learn what no single practice can. Studies across practices are planned, not running: nothing in Steady today sends data from one practice to another. This topic sets out what is already decided and what is still open, so that the design can be worked out here, together.

## Decided

- **Each practice's data stays on its own server.** Patients' information doesn't leave the practice that holds it.
- **Only totals and averages are shared, never individual records.** Counts, means and proportions: nothing about any one patient. The same rule holds on this forum.
- **Ethics approval first.** No study across practices starts without it.
- **Consent, and opt-in.** A patient's data is used in a study only with their consent, and each practice chooses whether to take part.
- **VR Clinical Psychology signs off on the science**, as it does for everything clinical in Steady. Kempie van Rooyen holds RADR's ethics approval and its consent for research use.
- **It comes later**, once practices have settled into using Steady in their care.

## Still open

- **Which questions first.** What would be worth a study? Some questions Steady's data could speak to: how check-in completion changes over months of therapy; how often questionnaire scores change between sessions; how closely the daily check-in follows the PHQ-9 and GAD-7; whether watch data adds anything to check-ins in South African practice.
- **The smallest group that can be reported.** A total from a handful of patients can still point to someone. What minimum group size should any shared number have, and what happens to small or rare categories?
- **Consent in practice.** What patients are told about each study, how they agree (in the app, on paper, or both), and how they withdraw.
- **Ethics in practice.** Which committee, and whether one approval can cover several practices.
- **Comparable data.** The same instrument versions and methods across practices. Steady's exports already state the methods behind their numbers (RADR's version, the flag rules, the Steady build), which helps.
- **How totals leave a server.** Since the data stays on each practice's server, the totals have to be worked out there. How that is done, checked and logged is for OpenWorks IT to build once the design is agreed.
- **Who does what.** Who designs a study, who analyses it, who writes it up and is named as an author, and how results come back to the practices and to patients.

## How to take part now

- Reply with a question you'd want answered across practices, and why it matters to your work.
- Add papers that should shape a study to The reading list.
- If you have sat on a research ethics committee, or been through one with a similar study, say so: that experience will be needed.
