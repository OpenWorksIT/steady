# Troubleshooting and where to get help

**The emailed code didn't arrive.** Check your spam folder. The code works for 15 minutes; if it has expired, tap **No email? Check spam, or start again for a new code** and sign in again.

**"That code didn't work" for my authenticator code.** Check that the time on your phone is set automatically, and use the newest code. Each code works once, so if you've just used one, wait for the next. If you've lost the phone, use a recovery code.

**I've lost my phone with my authenticator app.** Lock your account from the sign-in screen on any device (**Staff: lost your phone? Lock your account**), then contact OpenWorks IT. If you still have your recovery codes, you can sign in with one of those instead of a code.

**A patient forgot their password.** Open the patient and choose **Reset password** from the menu (or ask the practice administrator). Give them the temporary password by phone or in person.

**A patient's invite code doesn't work.** Each code works once and expires after seven days. Create a new one with **Invite**.

**I'm not getting notifications.** The web version can't send them. In the phone app, check **Settings > Notifications**, and allow notifications for Steady in the phone's settings.

**Steady keeps asking me to sign in again in the browser.** A browser session belongs to its tab and lasts while the tab is open. Closing the tab, or signing out on another device, means signing in again.

**I signed out on my computer and now my phone is signed out too.** **Sign out** signs you out on all your devices.

**A patient doesn't appear on my list.** They may be archived (**Settings > Archived patients**), their access may have been stopped, or they may be another psychologist's patient who hasn't been shared with you.

**Why is a patient at the top of my list?** Tap their flags or the ⓘ to see **Why [name] is here**. Mark flags as reviewed once you've dealt with them.

**The same flag appears twice on a patient.** Two questionnaires completed on different days (within the last 14 days) can give the same result. Open **Questionnaires and exercises** to see the dates.

**I can't send a questionnaire from the library.** It may still need a licence check: open it under **Needs a licence check** and confirm the practice may use it.

**I don't see the Community tab.** Your practice may not take part in the community yet, or you may be using the web version on an address other than your practice's own Steady address. It's for psychologists only.

**Can a patient see my notes or their scores?** No. Notes are for you and colleagues you share the patient with. Scores, bands and flags go only to the psychologist.

## Where to get help

Email OpenWorks IT at **info@openworks.co.za**.

You can also use the **Feedback** button in Steady to report a bug or suggest a change; it reaches OpenWorks IT and your practice's master psychologist and administrator. Please don't name patients in feedback or emails.
