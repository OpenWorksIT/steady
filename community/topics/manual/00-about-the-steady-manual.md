# About the Steady manual
<!-- steady: pinned -->

The manual for psychologists and practice administrators at practices that use Steady 1.1. It is in ten topics in this category:

1. About the Steady manual (this topic)
2. Getting started: signing in, two-step and your devices
3. Your patient list and inviting patients
4. A patient's page: flags, notes, watch data and RADR
5. Questionnaires, exercises, alerts and the library
6. Exports, sharing and deletion requests
7. Settings, feedback, the community and using a computer
8. For practice administrators and the master psychologist
9. What your patients see
10. Troubleshooting and where to get help

Ask about anything in a topic in its replies. OpenWorks IT reads and answers here; never post anything about a patient, here or anywhere in the community.

## Who this manual is for

This manual is for the people at a practice who use Steady at work:

- **Psychologists**, who invite patients, follow their check-ins, send questionnaires and exercises, and review what comes back (topics 2 to 7).
- **The master psychologist** (the practice's master clinician) and the **practice administrator**, who look after the practice's people, the audit report and the agreement with OpenWorks IT (topic 8).
- Anyone who needs to explain Steady to a patient (topic 9).

## About the screenshots

The screenshots come from Steady 1.1.0 running on a test server, at phone size and, in a few places, on a computer screen. The practice ("Demo Practice"), the staff (Dr Thandi Example, Dr Johan Sample, Lerato Example) and the patients are all made up. Patients called "DEMO A", "DEMO B" and "DEMO C" are fictional demo patients with made-up watch data; Steady labels them "Fictional demo patient: made-up data".

On your practice's Steady you'll see your practice's name and logo where these show Demo Practice. The phone apps use the same screens as the web version. Where something works only in the phone app, this manual says so.

## What you need

- Your work email address. Steady emails you a code each time you use a new phone or browser.
- A phone with an authenticator app, for two-step sign-in. Aegis and 2FAS are free and open source; Google Authenticator and Microsoft Authenticator work too.
- The Steady app on your phone, or a web browser on a computer, open at your practice's Steady address.
