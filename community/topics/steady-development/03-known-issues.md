# Known issues and workarounds
<!-- steady: pinned, wiki -->

Things in Steady and in this community that don't work as they should yet, and what to do in the meantime. This post is a wiki: anyone can edit it. Keep each entry to what happens, what to do instead, and where it stands. When you add one, also send it with the Feedback button in Steady, so OpenWorks IT has the details. No patient details here, as everywhere.

## Open

**On Android, the back button doesn't step back through forum pages.**
What to do: use the forum's own links, or tap Steady Community at the top to go back to the start. The refresh button on the Community tab ("Open it again") also starts afresh.
Status: known.

**In a web browser, the Community tab appears only on a steadyapp.co.za address.**
In a browser, the forum sits inside Steady's page and keeps its sign-in only when both are on the same site (such as vr.steadyapp.co.za and community.steadyapp.co.za). A practice whose web app is on another address doesn't get the tab in the browser.
What to do: use the Steady app on your phone.
Status: a limit of how browsers treat sign-ins inside another page.

**"The community didn't open here."**
The Community tab shows this when the forum asks it to sign you in again within a minute of the last time, usually because the browser won't keep the forum's sign-in.
What to do: tap Try again after a minute. If it keeps happening in a browser, use the phone app, and tell OpenWorks IT which browser it was.
Status: known.

## Working as intended

These can look like problems but aren't:

- **The forum opens only inside Steady.** Opened from a bookmark, or from a link in one of the forum's emails, it says it opens inside Steady. Open Steady and choose Community.
- **Practice admins and patients have no Community tab.** The community is for psychologists, with OpenWorks IT in two categories.
- **Notifications say only "New notification".** On purpose: nothing about a patient goes through Apple or Google.
- **RADR never alerts.** It orders your list, after answers waiting for review, and never uses alarm colours.
- **Patients don't see their questionnaire scores.** You talk them through.
- **A web session ends when you close the tab**, and after the computer has slept for longer than about 40 minutes to an hour.

Seen something that isn't here? Add it, or say so in a reply.
