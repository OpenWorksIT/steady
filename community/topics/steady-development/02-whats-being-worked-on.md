# What's being worked on

What OpenWorks IT is working on for Steady, as it stands. These are plans, not promises: there are no dates, and things can change. This post is kept up to date as the work moves.

## Now

- **Android notifications through Google.** Notifications on Android phones are moving to Google's push service (Firebase Cloud Messaging). What a notification says doesn't change: only "New notification", on every phone. Until it's in place on your phone, the Alerts tab shows everything new.
- **Steady in the Play Store and App Store.** Submitted, and in review with Google and Apple since 1 November. Until they're approved, Steady installs as it does now, through the practice's invite page.
- **The Steady manual.** A training and onboarding manual for psychologists and practice staff is being written.

## Later

- **Studies across practices.** Planned for later, with ethics approval and consent, and sharing only totals and averages, never individual records. How they will work is being discussed by the psychologists in Research and studies.
- **Watch-based alerts for selected high-risk patients.** To be explored later with VR Clinical Psychology, who sign off on Steady's science. Steady doesn't do this today: watch data raises no alerts, and nobody watches Steady around the clock.

## How things get onto this list

Mostly from Feedback tickets and from topics here. If something would make a real difference to your work, start a topic and say why. Others can add their voice, and that helps decide what comes first.
