# How to report a problem or ask for something
<!-- steady: pinned -->

Something in Steady not working, or not working the way you'd like? There are a few ways to say so, and each suits a different kind of thing.

## In the app: the Feedback button

The round **Feedback** button sits at the bottom left of the screen whenever you're signed in to Steady as staff. Choose **Love this**, **Don't like this** or **Bug found**, and say what you liked, what would make it better, or what went wrong and what you expected.

- A bug report can carry a screenshot of the screen you were on. Steady **blurs it** first, so no patient can be read from it, and you can leave it out.
- It also sends the screen you were on, the time and your last steps in the app (screens, taps and calls to the server), so OpenWorks IT can retrace what happened. Nothing you typed is included.
- It goes to OpenWorks IT and to your practice's master clinician and practice admin.
- You can follow it under Settings > Practice > **Feedback tickets**: Raised, Looked at or Done, with OpenWorks' note.

This is the best route for a bug: it carries the details that make it quick to find.

## Here, in Steady development

For ideas, questions, and anything other practices might share or want a say in. Start a topic, or reply to one. To make sure OpenWorks IT sees it, mention `@openworks`. If you've also sent a Feedback ticket, say so.

## Directly

- **When Steady isn't working for the practice at all**: info@openworks.co.za, or 076 768 9155 (phone or WhatsApp).
- **A security problem**: email info@openworks.co.za with "Steady security" in the subject. Please don't post it here.

## What to include

- what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead
- roughly when, and on what: phone or web, Android or iPhone
- Steady's version, at the bottom of Settings

The Feedback button adds most of this for you.

## What never to include

No patient names or details, and no screenshots or photos of patient screens: not here, not in a ticket's words, not in an email or a WhatsApp message. A screenshot you take yourself isn't blurred. Describe the screen instead: "a patient's RADR tab" or "the third patient in my list" is enough for OpenWorks to try it on a test server with fictional patients.

Is anything unclear about where to go? Ask in a reply.
