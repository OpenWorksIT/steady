# What's new in Steady 1.1

Steady 1.1 is the first large update since VR Clinical Psychology's own server started on 1.0.0 on 30 October. It is also the update that brings this community into Steady. Here is what you will notice, roughly in the order you will notice it.

## A shorter bottom bar

The bar now has three tabs: **Patients**, **Alerts** and **Community**. The gear at the top opens **Settings** as a page of its own, and **Settings > Practice** holds what used to be tabs, each for whoever had it before:

- **Library**: questionnaires, exercises and daily words (every psychologist)
- **People**: patients and staff, invites and access (the master clinician)
- **Audit report**: who opened or changed what (the master clinician and the practice admin)
- **Feedback tickets**: what the practice raised with the Feedback button, and where it stands
- **Operator agreement**: what the practice signed with OpenWorks IT (the master clinician and the practice admin)

## This community, inside Steady

The **Community** tab opens Steady Community as you, with the Steady sign-in you already use. There is no forum password. In a web browser, the tab appears only when Steady is on a steadyapp.co.za address, as VR's is.

## RADR, to improve care

RADR now explains itself. Tap any number, domain, day or flag and Steady shows what was measured, this person's usual range, the value then, how far it is from usual and why that counts, with the readings behind it. You can **mark a RADR flag as reviewed**, with an optional note, and it then shows who reviewed it and when.

The patient list keeps one order: answers waiting for review first (PHQ-9 item 9, the higher bands, low mood in the last three check-ins), then RADR, then everything else. Once you have reviewed what RADR showed, it stops moving that patient up your list until something new appears. Tap a patient's flags to see why they are where they are. How RADR works is in The science.

## Exports

The text PDF for note-taking tools is now called **Export for AI note-taking**. As before, it can hold only what's new since the last one. Every export now also lists RADR's flags and whether each was marked as reviewed. Once exported, the file is part of your clinical record.

## Notifications

Every notification now says only "New notification", on every phone: not even what kind of thing is new. Open Steady to see what it is. Notifications on Android phones are moving to Google's push service; see "What's being worked on" in Steady development.

## Consent

Steady now has one consent statement for every practice, signed off with Steady's science. Patients who agreed to the earlier draft are asked to read and agree once more the next time they open Steady, so you may get a question or two about it. Among other things, it says that patients of 12 and older who understand it agree for themselves, and that a parent or guardian agrees for a child under 12.

## Smaller things

- **Lock Steady**, for patients: an optional switch in their Settings that asks for the phone's own screen lock when Steady opens. The lock screen still shows the crisis numbers.
- The phone's app switcher now shows a blank picture of Steady, for everyone.
- **In a web browser**, your session belongs to the tab. Closing the tab signs you out there; reloading keeps you in, and you are never signed out in the middle of a session. A computer that sleeps for longer than about 40 minutes to an hour comes back to the sign-in screen.
- Settings shows Steady's version (1.1.0) where it used to show a code.
- The master clinician is asked to sign the operator agreement with OpenWorks IT in the app, the first time they open 1.1. Nobody else is asked.
