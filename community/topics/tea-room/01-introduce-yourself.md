Welcome to the Tea room, the community's staff room. A short hello here helps everyone know who is around and what they'd like to talk about. Reply to this topic with a few lines about yourself and your work. Copy the outline below if it helps, and leave out anything you'd rather not say.

```
Name, and what you like to be called:
Practice (it already shows beside your name):
Areas of work (who you see, what with, how you work):
Using Steady since (or "just starting"):
What I hope Steady helps with:
Something I'd like from this community:
Optional: a paper, book or idea that shaped how I work:
```

Other things people often mention:

- the languages you work in
- whether you supervise, or are in supervision
- research interests, and whether you'd like to take part in a study across practices later
- what you do when you're not working
- a question you'd like to ask the others

Short is fine. You can always add more later by editing your reply.

This topic is about you, not your patients, so no case details here. If you want to talk about a case, go to Clinical practice, and read the de-identifying checklist pinned there first.
