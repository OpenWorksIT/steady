# Books, podcasts and CPD worth sharing

Something you read, heard or attended that was worth the time? Share it here. It doesn't have to be about Steady, or even about between-session care: anything that made you a better clinician, or a more rested one, belongs here.

## What fits

- **Books**: clinical, professional or simply good. A novel that taught you something about people counts.
- **Podcasts, talks and lectures**: worth the commute.
- **CPD**: courses, workshops, webinars and conferences you'd recommend, or would warn others off. If it carried CPD points, say who accredited it, so others can check it counts for them.
- **Tools for the work around the work**: a way of keeping notes, planning a week or keeping up with the literature.

## A simple way to post one

- **What it is**, with a link if there is one
- **Why it was worth it**, in a line or two
- **Who it suits**: new to practice, supervisors, anyone who works with adolescents, and so on

Papers belong in The reading list, in Research and studies, where they can be found again later. Here, anything goes, as long as the usual rule holds: no patient details, even in passing ("it made me think of a client who..." is where that starts).
