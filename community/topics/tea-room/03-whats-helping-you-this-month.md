# What's helping you this month?

Clinical work asks a lot, and most of us are better at recommending rest than taking it. This topic is a place to say, briefly, what is helping you at the moment: with the workload, the admin, the evenings, or simply getting through a heavy week.

It can be small:

- a change to how you schedule your days, or where you put the admin
- a boundary that held, or one you are working on
- something you've stopped doing, and don't miss
- a walk, a book, a craft, a team, a garden
- a habit around checking messages or Steady outside working hours

Reply whenever you like, as often as you like. A line is enough. You don't need to reply to anyone else's, and nobody needs to give advice unless it's asked for.

Keep it general, as everywhere here: about you and your work, not about patients, and not about colleagues who could be recognised.

If things are heavier than a forum can hold, please talk to someone: your own therapist, your supervisor or a colleague you trust. SADAG's line, 0800 567 567, is free and open around the clock, for psychologists too.

So: what's helping you this month?
